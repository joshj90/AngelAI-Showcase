Project Overview

Angel AI is an experimental wearable intelligent assistant designed to explore the relationship between artificial intelligence, human-computer interaction, environmental awareness, and human performance.

The project investigates how multimodal sensing and context-aware artificial intelligence can provide relevant assistance while reducing unnecessary cognitive demands on users.

The long-term objective is to develop an intelligent companion capable of supporting individuals across civilian, professional, industrial, and government environments.

Research Objectives

Reduce cognitive load through context-aware assistance.

Improve environmental and situational awareness.

Explore multimodal sensor integration and intelligent information processing.

Investigate human-centered interaction with wearable artificial intelligence.

Support accessible and adaptive technology experiences.

Evaluate the relationship between intelligent assistance, task performance, and human decision-making.
